FAQ

Is the app in English?

Not yet — NotiPet's menus and windows are in Korean for now. The pet, bubbles and your notifications work the same in any language, and notification text is shown exactly as your apps send it.

How do I get a key?

Leave your email address in Request a key. We'll email you a 48-character key for that address. Enter the email and the key together in the window that appears the first time you open the app — it won't ask again.

Can I use it for free?

Yes — version 1.x is free with no key (10 pets). Pick a 1.x version from the download list. The 5 pets added in 2.2 are only in 2.x.

How many Macs can one key be used on?

Up to 4 Macs (from 2.4.1). A Mac registers once over the internet the first time you enter the key. On a 5th Mac you'll see the list of registered Macs — choose 🐾 menu › Unregister this Mac on one you no longer use to free a slot. Once registered, a Mac keeps working offline.

The bubble shows up a little after the banner

macOS takes a few seconds to store each notification, so bubbles appear about 5 seconds after the system banner. That's normal.

I see both the system banner and the bubble

The banner comes from macOS, so NotiPet can't turn it off. To see only the bubble, set each app's alert style to None in System Settings › Notifications, and keep Show in Notification Center on.

Notifications from one app don't show up

Check that the app's notifications are on in System Settings › Notifications, and that it isn't in the menu's muted apps. Pop-ups an app draws inside its own window aren't macOS notifications, so NotiPet can't see them.

It still says it needs permission after I turned it on

Remove NotiPet from the Full Disk Access list with −, add it back with +, then quit and reopen NotiPet. Do this once after updating from an older version, too.

How do I update or uninstall?

Update: from 2.4.0 and 1.5.0 on, the pet tells you when a new version is out — click and it updates itself (1.x stays within 1.x). From older versions, replace the app with the new DMG once and turn Full Disk Access on again. Going from 1.x to 2.0+ needs a key once. Uninstall: menu › Quit → move NotiPet to the Trash → remove it from the Full Disk Access list with −. To also erase settings, run defaults delete kr.sanghak.notipet in Terminal.
