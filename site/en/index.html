FAQ

Is the app in English?

Not yet. Menus are in Korean for now.

Bubbles show your notifications as-is.

How do I get a key?

Leave your email in Request a key.

We'll email you a key for it.

Enter the email and key in the app.

Is there a free version?

Yes. 1.x is free, no key (10 pets).

The 5 new pets are 2.x only.

How many Macs per key?

Up to 4 Macs.

Free a slot: Unregister this Mac.

Registered Macs work offline.

Bubbles come a bit late

About 5 seconds after the banner.

That's how long macOS takes to save it.

I see a banner and a bubble

macOS shows the banner, not NotiPet.

Set the alert style to None.

Keep Notification Center on.

One app's alerts are missing

Check that app's notifications are on.

Check the muted apps in the menu.

In-app pop-ups can't be read.

It keeps asking for permission

Remove it with −, add it with +.

Then quit and reopen NotiPet.

How do I update?

The pet tells you when one is out.

Click the bubble and it updates.

1.x only updates within 1.x.

How do I uninstall?

Menu › Quit, then move it to Trash.

Remove it from Full Disk Access too.
